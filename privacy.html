隱私權政策（繁體中文）

本工具只存取帳號擁有者本人的 Google 雲端硬碟，用途限於備份與同步本人的檔案。本工具不蒐集、不儲存、不分析、也不分享任何第三方的資料；檔案內容只存在於帳號擁有者本人的 Google 帳戶與本人的電腦之間，不會傳送到任何其他伺服器，也不會用於廣告或任何形式的轉售。本工具不使用 Cookie、不做追蹤、不建立使用者帳號。存取權限由 Google OAuth 授權取得，帳號擁有者可隨時在 Google 帳戶的「第三方應用程式與服務存取權」頁面撤銷授權；撤銷後本工具即無法再存取任何雲端硬碟資料。

Privacy Policy (English)

This tool accesses only the account owner's own Google Drive, and only in order to back up and sync that owner's files. It does not collect, store, analyse or share any third party's data. File contents stay between the account owner's own Google account and the owner's own computer; nothing is transmitted to any other server, and nothing is used for advertising or resale of any kind. The tool sets no cookies, performs no tracking, and creates no user accounts. Access is granted through Google OAuth, and the account owner may revoke it at any time on the Google Account page for third-party apps and services with account access; once revoked, the tool can no longer reach any Drive data.
